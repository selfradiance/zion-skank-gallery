Meet Zion

Same bones, new world every time

Zion Skank is the face of James's reggae. Each film drops him into a new art style and a new fusion of reggae with something else, but a handful of things never change, and every prompt carries them.

  • FaceA grinning skull, always smiling. When he sings, the jaw opens; no lips, no tongue.
  • ShadesRed sunglasses, always on. His eyes are never shown.
  • HairLong brown dreadlocks.
  • HeadphonesRed, gold and green, around his neck.
  • ShoesRed high-top sneakers.
  • MoodPlayful and positive. Spooky-cute at most, never gory.

JAH JAH
BLUES

A little felt rain cloud called the Blues follows Zion Skank around a gray felt town. Every chorus he dances, colour stitches back into the streets, and by the end the cloud is dancing too, raining on a garden.

How it was made

In the order it happened
Step 1

The song

James wanted reggae as the backbone with a jump-blues kick. The first draft leaned too blues, so the second kept the same lyrics in Jamaican patois and put roots reggae first in the style prompt. Suno ran long at 3:07 and James faded the ending by hand.

Conscious roots reggae fused with upbeat jump blues: a deep, bass-forward one-drop reggae groove carrying exuberant 1940s horn-swing energy … Soulful, gravelly older Jamaican male lead vocal singing in patois, full of warmth and humor; joyful male backing group with big call-and-response choruses.

It opens on a knock at the door:

Knock knock! Who dat? Jah Jah Blues! Come een, come een, wi have room fi yuh!
Step 2

The cast

Zion in stitched felt, the Blues cloud (big four-hole button eyes, no arms, yarn rain), three skeleton neighbours (no sunglasses: red shades belong to Zion alone) and a town that is half gray, half colour.

Step 3

Fourteen boards

Each board is one image holding eight 16:9 stills, written against a timing map of the song so every board covers its own stretch of music. Zion stays the only colour until the choruses start bringing it back.

Step 4

Motion and lip sync

Every board became a short motion clip, and ten singing shots were made from the song's own vocal so Zion's felt jaw opens on the words.

Step 5

The edit

Claude cut the clips to the song and added the lyrics and style layer. Version 2 fixed a glitching door arm and a lip-sync stretch around 0:38 to 0:44. Four vertical shorts were cut from it too.
